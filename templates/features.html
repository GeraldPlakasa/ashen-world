GAME SYSTEMS
Detailed Documentation

  • Auto-generation of 50 initial villagers with randomized stats
  • Gender-based system (Male/Female)
  • Age progression: 1 year = 90 in-game days
  • Job assignment from 50+ occupations
  • Trait system (~20 traits affecting behavior)
  • Family name inheritance system
  • Immigration system (new villagers can join)

Core Actions
  • Train — ATK/DEF
  • Study — INT
  • Work — Coins (taxed)
  • Rest — HP
  • Buy Food — Hunger
Social / Risk
  • Buy Gear — Stat items
  • Hunt — Combat
  • Socialize — +Relationship
  • Hangout — Group social
  • Steal — Risk action

  • Hunt system with procedural enemies (common, elite, legendary)
  • Enemy scaling based on villager level
  • Combat resolution with win/loss/death outcomes
  • Experience and leveling system
  • Starvation damage mechanics

  • Dynamic scores (-100 to +120)
  • Labels: friend, bestfriend, love, rival, enemy
  • Relationship-based actions
  • Decay/growth over time

  • Love threshold triggers marriage
  • Spouse system with mutual tracking
  • Breakup mechanics
  • Same-family marriage prohibited
  • Widowing when spouse dies

Birth Logic
  • Mothers 18–42, fathers 18–65
  • Birth cooldown: 45 days
  • Probability by love score
Children (0–16)
  • Immune to hunger
  • Limited actions
  • Stat/trait inheritance

  • Estate distribution on death
  • Priority: children → spouse → family → treasury
  • Player character succession

King System
  • Elections every 5 years
  • Emergency elections on death
  • Term limits: 3 max
  • Dynasty bonus
Assassination
  • Daily chance to assassinate enemies
  • Influenced by traits + buildings

  • Village treasury system
  • Dynamic tax rates (0–35%)
  • Tax on work and hunt income
  • Corruption mechanics

  • 🏪 Marketplace — work income
  • 📚 Library — study boost
  • ⚔️ Barracks — training
  • 🌾 Granary — hunger
  • 🏥 Clinic — survival
  • 🧱 Walls — defense
  • 🛕 Temple — REP gains
  • 🔨 Blacksmith — gear
  • 🏛️ Treasury — interest
  • 👑 Royal Court — prestige
  • 📊 Tax Office — tax rate
  • 🍺 Tavern — social
Mechanics
  • 3 levels per building
  • Health system (0–100%) with decay
  • Auto-repair below 60%

  • Children can't work until age 17
  • Marriage age: 18–60
  • Graveyard archival after 25 years dead
  • One player character per user
  • King term limits: 3 lifetime

  • Brave → more train/hunt
  • Cautious → more study
  • Greedy → more work/steal
  • Generous → more socialize
  • Wise → more study
  • Hot-headed → more combat
  • Diligent → more work/train
  • Lazy → more rest
  • …and more

  • 30+ item types across 3 tiers
  • Tier 1 (common): Weapon, Armor, Tome
  • Tier 2 (rare): Vitality Belt, Mageweave
  • Tier 3 (legendary): Dragonbone, Voidsteel
  • Permanent stat bonuses
  • Level-based tier weighting
