Simulation Overview

Ashen World runs a continuous village simulation where each day advances automatically or manually.

Time System
  • 90 days = 1 year
  • Auto-simulation: 1 real second per day
  • Manual advancement available
  • Year rollover triggers aging, elections, stats
Daily Cycle
  • Each villager chooses an action (producers deposit resources)
  • Actions resolve (combat, work, social)
  • Village eats from the stockpile (adults' hunger drops)
  • King may import resources from outside if stockpile is low
  • Buildings constructed, upgraded, decayed, repaired
  • Births (throttled by food supply), deaths, marriages processed
  • World events may trigger; chronicle records narrative

Elder Decay (Age 70+)

A daily phase applies graduated aging effects to villagers past 70. HP attrition, occasional stat decay (random one of ATK/DEF/INT loses 1-2), MP fade for magic users, and a small chance of "passed away peacefully" each day.

Combat System

Combat occurs during hunting, quests, invasions, and assassinations. Outcomes depend on effective stats (base + equipped artifact mods), gear, magic, and a logistic win curve.

Character Power Formula
  • power = ATK×1.15 + DEF + INT×0.6 + level×12 + REP×0.2 + 20
  • +HP mod from artifacts: ×0.15 added to power (mirrors enemy HP-power term)
  • Magic jobs: add spell_power = MP×0.8 + INT×0.4 on top, spends 30-60% of effective MP
  • Minor magic jobs: add MP×0.3 + INT×0.15, spends 15-30% MP
  • MAGIC-tagged skills: +15% combat power per skill (stacks)
Hunting
  • Enemy tier scales with villager level (weak / standard / elite / legendary)
  • Win probability via logistic curve on (char_power - enemy_power)
  • Win rewards: coins, EXP, food (hunger reduction), small REP gain
  • Loss risk: HP damage scaled by margin; HP ≤ 0 = death
  • Scratch damage on win: ~20% of damage variance even when victorious
  • Artifact drop chance: 0.1% common · 0.7% elite · 8% legendary kills
Building Bonuses
  • Temple Lvl N: +N REP gain on hunt wins
  • Barracks Lvl N: EXP × (1 + 0.10×N)
  • Market Lvl N: coin reward × (1 + 0.05×N)
  • Walls Lvl N: reduces casualties during invasion events
  • Clinic Lvl N: raises plague survival, faster post-combat HP recovery
Gear & Artifacts
  • Equip slots: weapon, armor, ring, amulet, tome
  • Auto-equip on artifact pickup if slot empty or strictly higher rarity
  • effective_stats = base + sum of equipped mods (base stays untouched)
  • Bought gear (Buy Gear action): +ATK or +DEF directly on base stats
  • Soulbound items destroyed when owner dies (chronicled)
Magical Artifacts & Inheritance

Rare items with stat bonuses that persist after death and pass to heirs. Each artifact accumulates a lineage — a chronicle of past owners — so an old sword in Year 30 carries the names of the four kings who carried it.

Where They Come From
  • Combat drops — 0.1% common · 0.7% elite · 8% legendary kills. Rarity heavily weighted toward common; legendary items are vanishingly rare.
  • Quest rewards — ARCANE success = guaranteed legendary tome. Other quests up to ~12% chance, weighted by quest type and party margin.
  • Ancient Tomb event — very rare world event; awards 1–2 artifacts to top-rep villagers (Library can bump the count).
  • Forge action — only high-INT crafters who clear a strict checklist can attempt one (see prerequisites below).
  • Trade Caravan event — rare; merchant offers an artifact at steep treasury cost (600 / 1800 / 5000 / 15000g).
  • Equip slots: weapon, armor, ring, amulet, tome — auto-equip if empty or upgrade by rarity.
Stat Application
  • effective_stats(v) = base stats + sum of equipped artifact mods
  • Used by combat resolution; base stats stay untouched
  • +HP mods give a small combat-power bonus (mirrors enemy HP-power term)
  • Effective MP drives spell power; only the base MP pool is spent
  • Effective DEF mitigates incoming damage
Forge Action — Full Prerequisite Checklist

Forging an artifact is a major undertaking. Every condition below must hold, and even when it does, ~40% of attempts produce nothing (materials lost):

  • Job: Blacksmith, Wizard, or Sorcerer
  • INT ≥ 50, Level ≥ 15, Age ≥ 25
  • HP ≥ 70, Hunger ≤ 60 — must be in good physical shape
  • Treasury ≥ 800g; cost per attempt is 600–1000g (more for INT≥80)
  • Building: Blacksmith ≥ Lvl 1 (smiths) or Library ≥ Lvl 2 (mages)
  • Skill: at least one CRAFT or KNOWLEDGE skill (smiths) / MAGIC or KNOWLEDGE skill (mages)
  • Cooldown: 365 days (one full year) since the villager's last forge
  • Output: 80% common / 18% uncommon / 2% rare. INT ≥ 80 shifts to 55/40/5. Never legendary, never soulbound.
  • Smiths produce weapons & armor; mages also produce rings & tomes.
Inheritance Flow (Settle on Death)
  1. Soulbound items are destroyed with the owner — chronicled as a loss.
  2. Otherwise, an heir is chosen in order: oldest living child → surviving spouse → oldest living sibling.
  3. If no heir exists, the artifact is liquidated to the treasury for a rarity-scaled payout (50 / 150 / 500 / 2000 coins for common / uncommon / rare / legendary).
  4. Each transfer appends an entry to the artifact's forged_history — its permanent chronicle of past owners.
  5. The chronicle records the inheritance event; legendary transfers run at importance 4.

World Events (10 Types)

Random events occur once per year, affecting the entire village.

Negative Events
  • Plague - HP damage, deaths. Clinic reduces impact. Clerics/Healers resist 50% better (spend MP).
  • Famine - Hunger spikes village-wide, drains 30–55% of the food stockpile, and bleeds some treasury. Granary mitigates all three.
  • Invasion - Combat event. Walls/Barracks defend. Magic users cast defensive spells (MP drain).
  • Storm - Building damage.
Positive Events
  • Festival - Happiness, relationship boosts.
  • Good Harvest - Reduces hunger, gains +200–400 food into the stockpile (×Granary/Market bonus), and adds coin bonus to the treasury. Nature jobs earn extra coin.
  • Blessing - HP and MP restoration for all.
  • Windfall - Treasury bonus.
  • Ancient Tomb - Rare. 1–3 top-rep villagers each take home an artifact (Library boosts count).
  • Trade Caravan - Rare. King may spend treasury to acquire an artifact (Market reduces price).
Diseases & Healers

Persistent per-villager illness, separate from the yearly Plague event. Diseases spread via daily contact (work, socialize, spouse cohabitation), drain HP each day, and can kill if untreated. Recovery grants permanent immunity to that specific disease.

Disease Catalog (3 tiers)

🤒 Fever20% / 35%10 days3–61.2%/day past midpoint40%
☠️ Plague Boils12% / 20%14 days5–94.5%/day past midpoint25%

Recovery rolls each day after onset (cough 20% / fever 12% / plague 6%, +20% after full duration). Clinic adds a passive +4%/level to natural recovery for everyone.

Ambient Infection
  • Each alive villager has a 0.25% chance per day to catch something from outside (immigrants, traders, bad water).
  • Pool weights: cough 65% / fever 27% / plague 8%.
  • Already-immune villagers can't be re-infected with the same disease.
Healers (Cure)
  • Healer jobs: Healer, Cleric, Herbalist, Priest.
  • Use the Heal Sick action — they prefer spouse → family → anyone sick.
  • Cure chance = disease base + (INT/25 × 10%, cap +40%) + Clinic ×8%/lvl. Clerics & Priests get +5%/lvl of Temple.
  • On success: patient gains immunity to that disease; healer earns +REP and a 2–8g tip.
Crime & Justice

Villagers can commit theft, assault, or murder. Witnessed crimes become pending court cases on the village docket; if there is a sitting King, he rules on each case once per day. Verdicts and reports appear in the Chronicle under the justice category.

Crime Catalog
CrimeSeverityBase witnessFineNotes
Theft (Steal)1 (petty)10%30gMost common. Rep -4 to -10. Mutual relationship -20 to -40.
Assault2 (serious)25%60gDeals real HP damage. Lethal assaults are escalated to a murder charge.
Murder4 (grave)40%150gPremeditated. Targets the deepest rival. Survivors of murder attempts → assault charge.

Witness chance += +4% per alive Guard and +10% per Guard who chose Patrol today (max 95%). Each Guard's daily patrol acts as a multiplier on the village's law-enforcement coverage.

How a crime becomes a trial
  1. A villager commits Steal / Assault / Murder via the daily action loop.
  2. A witness check rolls — modulated by alive Guards and same-day Patrollers.
  3. If witnessed: case added to bank.pending_crimes; a "X accused of..." chronicle entry is published.
  4. Later in the same tick (after the King-assassination phase), the King issues verdicts on every pending case at once.
  5. No sitting King → the docket carries forward until an election seats one.
Guard Jobs & Deterrence
  • Recognized law enforcement: Guard, Captain, Soldier, Commander.
  • Each alive Guard reduces the assault weight in villagers' action choices (×0.6 at ≥2 guards, ×0.5 at ≥5).
  • At ≥3 guards, murder weight ×0.4 — strong deterrent for would-be killers.
  • Patrol action: +1-2 DEF / +1-2 REP / +EXP. Bonuses scale with City Walls and Barracks levels.
Yearly Ledger

Every witnessed crime and every verdict increments a per-year counter (crimes_committed, trials_held, fines_collected, exiles, executions). These power three surfaces:

  • Leaderboard / Past Reigns: each archived year now shows its crimes-witnessed count and a "Justice" line summarizing fines / exiles / executions.
  • Admin → Justice tab: KPI strip (this year + all-time), a stacked yearly chart of verdict mix overlayed with a crimes-witnessed line, plus a live feed of the latest justice-category chronicle entries.
  • Chronicle: all crimes and verdicts appear under the justice category — filterable on the Chronicle page.
Verdict Logic — what the King decides

Base verdict weights are severity-driven and then shifted by the King's traits and the defendant's prior record (recidivism).

CrimeFineExileExecution
Theft (first offense)88%10%2%
Assault (first offense)60%30%10%
Murder (first offense)15%50%35%

Recidivism: each prior conviction shifts +12% from fine → exile. At 3+ priors, mass bleeds from exile → execution. A 4-time petty thief is more likely to be exiled than fined.

King Trait Effects
  • Greedy: +20% fine — the treasury wins
  • Empathic / Generous: +15% fine, -8% exile, -7% execution
  • Strict: +7% exile, +3% execution
  • Cruel / Reckless / Hot-headed: +10% execution
  • Wise / Patient: +8% fine — measured justice
  • Cautious: +10% fine — avoids extreme verdicts
Verdict Outcomes
  • Fine: coins transferred from criminal → treasury (whatever they can pay). REP -5.
  • Exile: villager removed from the village (alive=False, distinct cause). REP -10. Family tree still records them.
  • Execution: public death by the King's order. REP -15. Chronicle importance 5.
  • Each verdict stamps the criminal's crime_record — used by future trials for recidivism scaling.
  • If the criminal died before trial, the case is marked died_before_trial and quietly closed.

Relationship Factors
  • Same family: +20 base bonus
  • Same job guild: +10 bonus
  • Socializing / hangout: +1 to +15 per interaction (depends on traits)
  • Stealing from someone: -20 to -40 mutual penalty

Family System
Marriage — Eligibility
  • Age: both partners 18-60
  • Gender: Male-Female pairs only
  • Not same family: different family names required
  • Both unmarried and alive
  • Mutual love score ≥ 85 (relationship label "love" requires score ≥ 80 in M-F pair; raised threshold of 85 enforces stronger bond before proposal)
  • Singles ranked by age desc, then rep desc — older/famous propose first
Marriage — Probability
  • Base proposal chance: 15% / day once eligible
  • Scales up to 35% for very high love score (+0.5% per point above 100)
  • Spouse becomes Queen automatically if marrying the King
  • Divorce: ~0.0005% / day (≈0.18% / year). Caps at 0.03% / day even at deeply broken relationships.
  • Marriage records appear in the Chronicle (importance 3)
Children — Birth Rules
  • Mother age: 18-50
  • Father age: 18-75
  • Cooldown: 45 days after a previous birth (per parent). A Midwife House shortens this by 8 days per level (min 20).
  • Base chance: 0.8% / day per couple
  • Love bonus: +0.2% if love ≥ 100, +0.1% if 80-99, -0.1% otherwise
  • Couple decay: ×0.65 per existing shared child (5th kid is rare)
  • Family decay: ×0.85 per child already born to the dad's family
  • Food-pressure throttle: ×0.6 at <7 days supply, ×0.3 at <4 days, ×0.1 at <2 days. Hungry villages stop growing.
  • Hard cap: 3% / day
Children — Inheritance at Birth
  • Family name: father's family
  • Stats: averaged from parents × ratio + small base roll (~12% for ATK/DEF, ~14% for INT) + ±1-2 noise
  • Newborn HP: 35-55 base, raised by +5 per Midwife House level (up to 50-70 at Lv 3 — fewer infant deaths from disease & starvation)
  • Traits: 1 from each parent + 8% chance of one random mutation
  • Skills: rare birth roll, parent skills bias the chance (see Skill section)
  • Royal Blood: +1 flag and +20 REP only for male children of a royal parent or the reigning King (Salic-style male-line succession — daughters do not inherit the title)
  • Generation (gen): max(mom.gen, dad.gen) + 1
Childhood (Age 0-16)
  • Job: "Child" — no work income, no hunger drain, no stockpile food consumption
  • Cannot marry, vote, or be recruited for quests
  • Daily actions: train, study, rest, hangout, socialize
  • At age 17 — Coming of Age job assignment priority:
    1. Strong skill affinity: 75% chance to follow it.
    2. Parent's trade (apprenticeship): 55% chance if father is a producer (Farmer/Miner/etc.), 35% otherwise. Falls back to mother's job if father lacks one.
    3. Stats + traits weighted random — producer jobs get +1.5 baseline weight so the village's producer ratio doesn't drift over generations.
  • New adult MP: 60-120 (magic job), 15-40 (minor magic), 0-10 (other)
Inheritance on Death
  • Coins: oldest living child(ren) → surviving spouse → adult kin (same family, age 17+) → treasury
  • Equally distributed with remainder to first heirs
  • Artifacts: see "Magical Artifacts" — soulbound items destroyed; others to oldest child / spouse / sibling, then liquidated
  • Family name persists through children
  • Royal Blood persists in descendants — once granted, it cascades down the line

Elections & Leadership
Schedule & Eligibility
  • Frequency: every 5 game years (450 sim-days)
  • Voters: all alive villagers age 16+
  • Candidates: alive males age 18+ (falls back to all adults if no males)
  • Term limit: max 3 consecutive terms per villager (lifetime king count is uncapped)
  • If everyone is term-limited, the candidate with fewest consecutive terms is chosen
  • Win percentage is computed against eligible voters (alive adults 16+), not the whole population — so the number matches the ballot box.
Leadership Score Formula
  • INT × 1.2 + REP × 0.9 + level × 2.0 + ATK × 0.4 + DEF × 0.5
  • Age shaping: +5 for ages 22-55, -8 for >55, -12 for 18-21, -1000 for <18
  • Trait bonuses: Wise +14, Loyal/Generous +8, Brave/Empathic/Diligent +6, Patient +5, Ambitious +3, Cautious +2
  • Trait penalties: Deceitful -12, Greedy/Lazy -10, Hot-headed -8, Reckless -6
  • Dynasty bonus: +30 if same family as previous King (was 1000 — so dominant it forced unanimous re-elections)
  • Voter affinity per (voter, candidate) pair: shared traits +4 each, opposing traits −4 each, same family +8, job/trait loyalty +2.5 to +3, plus a per-voter "mood" 0–25 random — wide enough that the top 3-4 candidates genuinely split the vote.
Tie-Breaking (in order)
  1. Higher leadership score
  2. Higher REP
  3. Higher INT
  4. Higher level
  5. Closeness to age 35 (older wins on ties)
  6. Coin flip
Emergency Elections
  • Triggered the same day the King dies
  • Same scoring + voting; does not count toward consecutive-term limit
  • Logged with importance 5 in the Chronicle (importance 4 if king died of natural causes)
Royal Blood Cascade
  • The moment a new king is crowned (not on re-election), the cascade walks only the male descendants: sons, grandsons through sons, and so on — flagged blue_blood = 1
  • Daughters do not receive the flag and their own children are skipped entirely — royal lineage is strictly male-line (Salic-style succession)
  • Each newly-flagged descendant earns the Royal Blood achievement and +20 REP
  • The king himself is not flagged — only the line below them
  • Future male children born while the parent is reigning King also inherit it
Election Aftermath — Runners-Up & Resentment

Losing candidates don't vanish from history. The top 3 non-winners with at least 1 vote are recorded in the chronicle entry as "Other contenders: X of the Y family (N votes); …", and accrue real consequences for the loser.

  • Streak tracking: each loss increments consecutive_losses on the villager. Winning resets it to 0.
  • Resentment hit: losing candidate's relationship with the winner drops by 10 + 15·(their_votes / winner_votes) + 3·prior_streak — close races feel robbed, repeat losers compound. A smaller reciprocal hit (¼ size) goes winner → loser.
  • REP bleed: losers lose −streak REP per defeat (publicly bad PR).
  • Action log: "lost election to X (streak N)" appears in their last_action field so the admin table reads the political moment.
  • Public rivalry brewing: at streak ≥ 3, a chronicle entry under royal appears with importance scaling 3 / 4 / 5 by streak length — flavour text in the spirit of "next time, by other means."
Rival Coup (Streak ≥ 2)

A perennial runner-up may stop playing the ballot game and reach for the dagger. Runs daily after king_assassination_phase; a successful coup kills the king and triggers an emergency election the next tick.

  • Eligibility: alive adult (age 18+), not King/Queen, consecutive_losses ≥ 2. No hard relationship gate — election noise scatters grudges across many former kings, so the streak itself is the anchor; current relationship just sweetens the chance.
  • Per-day base chance: 0.5% + 0.5%·(streak − 1) + 0.1%·(grudge/10), clamped to 4% max.
  • Trait modifiers: Ambitious +1.0%, Hot-headed +0.8%, Deceitful +0.6%, Brave +0.3%; Loyal −0.8%, Cautious −0.5%, Empathic −0.4%, Generous −0.3%.
  • Defences: each Barracks level −0.2%, each Walls level −0.1%.
  • Target: the most-motivated rival (longest streak, then deepest grudge against the current king).
  • On success: king dies ("slain in a coup"), rival's streak resets to 0, rival takes −25 REP, and the chronicle logs a scandal-category entry (importance 5) explicitly referencing the streak so a reader can follow the build-up to the act.

What a sitting king actually does — taxes, spending, trade, decrees, corruption, assassination risk — is consolidated in the next section, The King — Powers & Duties.

The King — Powers & Duties

Everything a sitting King can do, decide, or risk. How a King is chosen lives in Elections; this section is about the throne after coronation.

Standing Powers (Every Reign)
  • Sets tax policy — daily rate modulated by dominant traits (see formulas below).
  • Issues quests every 3 years (QUEST_INTERVAL_YEARS); king's traits influence quest type.
  • Construction & upgrades — automatically authorised when treasury + stockpile have enough; choice biased by traits.
  • Repairs — when a building drops below 60% health.
  • Foreign trade — imports food/materials when stockpile is low; exports surplus for coin. See full tables below.
  • Spouse becomes Queen automatically; lineage cascades to male descendants (Royal Blood).
Behaviour & Risks
  • Corruption — Greedy/Deceitful kings siphon treasury daily (costs 3–10 REP per theft).
  • Treasury floor — never spends imports below 300g (safety reserve).
  • Assassination — chance scales with REP loss + count of relationship-enemies.
  • Term limit — max 3 consecutive terms; lifetime king count is uncapped.
  • Job on dethronement — kings who lose their last election are reassigned a regular job by traits + stats.
  • Death — natural or assassinated triggers an emergency election the same day.
Tax Policy
  • Base rate: 10%; King traits modify ±8% total.
  • Tax Office: +1% per level; Treasury: +0.5% per level.

Foreign Trade — IMPORTS

When the stockpile runs low, the king may spend treasury coins to import resources from outside the village. Driven by traits, gated by a treasury floor of 300g.

  • Buy prices per unit: food = 1g, wood = 2g, stone = 3g, iron = 6g.
  • Food emergency < 1000: any king imports automatically to refill toward ~1500.
  • Soft trigger (food < 5 days supply or material < threshold): trait-modulated chance.
  • Trait modifiers (food): Generous/Empathic +35%, Cautious +25%, Wise +15%, Loyal +10%, Greedy −35%, Reckless −15%.
  • Trait modifiers (materials): Ambitious +30%, Diligent +20%, Protective +15% (stone/iron), Wise +10%, Greedy −25%.
Foreign Trade — EXPORTS

When a resource is well above its safety floor, the king may sell the surplus to outside merchants for coin. Sell prices are below buy prices (no arbitrage). The king never sells food below the export floor of 3000.

  • Sell prices per unit: food = 1g, wood = 1g, stone = 2g, iron = 4g.
  • Export floors (must EXCEED to sell): food 3000, wood 250, stone 200, iron 40.
  • Order sizes: 400 food / 100 wood / 70 stone / 30 iron per transaction. Bounded so a single sale never drops below the floor.
  • Food sell-chance (base 20%): Greedy +45%, Ambitious +20%, Deceitful +15%, Generous/Empathic −30%, Cautious −30%, Loyal −15%, Wise −5%.
  • Material sell-chance (base 25%): Greedy +40%, Deceitful +15%, Ambitious +10%, Diligent −10%, Cautious −15%, Protective −20% (stone/iron).

All imports & exports are chronicled under the economy category — visible in the admin Resources tab.

Militarization Decree (One-Shot, On Taking Office)

Each newly-crowned king (not re-elections) decides once whether the village needs a stronger garrison. If they choose "yes," they immediately conscript a handful of civilian adults into Guard or Scout duty — no opt-out, regardless of the conscript's current job. If they choose "no," nothing happens and the matter is closed for their entire reign.

  • Base chance: 12% — deliberately low so most kings stay civilian.
  • Trait modifiers: Protective +20%, Brave +20%, Hot-headed +15%, Ambitious +10%, Stoic +5%; Generous −25%, Empathic −20%, Wise −15%, Cautious −15%, Loyal −10%. Clamped to [0%, 70%].
  • Conscript count: 2–4 base, +1 each for Brave / Hot-headed / Ambitious. Capped by the eligible pool.
  • Exempt: King, Queen, and anyone already serving (Soldier/Guard/Captain/Commander/Scout/Falconer/Spy). Healers and magic users are not exempt — the king "brooks no excuse."
  • Conversion split: roughly even Guard/Scout assignment. Drafted villagers carry "conscripted into the Guard/Scout" in their last_action.
  • Logged in the Chronicle under royal with importance 4. Only fires once per reign — re-elections don't trigger it.

Resources (Shared Stockpile)

The village maintains a single shared stockpile of raw materials alongside its coin treasury. Producer jobs deposit into it daily; construction draws from it; and adults eat from it daily, lowering their hunger.

The Four Resources
  • Food — eaten every day. Stockpile feeds adults directly: hunger drops ~11/day when fed (Granary boosts).
  • Wood — primary building material for civic structures.
  • Stone — needed by walls, temples, treasuries, heavy structures.
  • Iron — scarce, needed by Barracks, Walls, Blacksmith, Royal Court, Treasury, and every Production building. Also consumed continuously by Blacksmiths — see the producer-jobs table below.
Daily Food Consumption
  • Each adult eats 1 food/day. Children eat 0 — they're fed implicitly by their family.
  • Granary (Lv ≥ 1) reduces total need to ×0.75 AND adds +2 hunger reduction per level when fed.
  • Stockpile meets demand → adults lose ~11 hunger/day. Stockpile short → fed-ratio scaling + a deficit hunger penalty.
  • Birth rate auto-throttles when food is low (×0.6 at <7 days supply, ×0.3 at <4, ×0.1 at <2 days).

Stockpile Pressure (producers react automatically)
  • Food < 1000 (Emergency): all producers get work +1.5; leisure (socialize/hangout/tavern/study) heavily dampened.
  • Food 1000–1500 (Cautious): producers get work +0.6; hangout lightly suppressed.
  • Food > 1500 (Stable): normal baseline. Hungry individual producers still get an extra work +1.5.

Initial Stockpile (Reset World)

Fresh world starts at 1500 food / 200 wood / 100 stone / 0 iron. Initial population spawns with 45% producer-job bias (~44 of 98 villagers); children inherit a parent's job ~55% of the time when they come of age (35% for non-producer parents), so the producer ratio doesn't dilute over generations.
