🎮 Simulation Overview

Ashen World runs a continuous village simulation where each day advances automatically or manually.

Time System
  • 90 days = 1 year
  • Auto-simulation: 1 real second per day
  • Manual advancement available
  • Year rollover triggers aging, elections, stats
Daily Cycle
  • Each villager chooses an action
  • Actions resolve (combat, work, social)
  • Births, deaths, marriages processed
  • World events may trigger

⚔️ Combat System

Combat occurs during hunting and invasion events. Outcomes depend on stats and gear.

Hunting
  • Enemy tier scales with villager level
  • Base damage = ATK - Enemy DEF
  • Critical hits possible (2x damage)
  • Rewards: coins, EXP, food
  • Risk: HP loss, possible death
Gear Effects
  • Weapon: +ATK bonus
  • Armor: +DEF bonus
  • Gear purchased via "Buy Gear" action
  • Blacksmith building improves quality

🌪️ World Events (8 Types)

Random events occur once per year, affecting the entire village.

⚠️ Negative Events
  • Plague - HP damage, deaths. Clinic reduces impact.
  • Famine - Hunger increase. Granary helps.
  • Invasion - Combat event. Walls/Barracks defend.
  • Storm - Building damage.
✨ Positive Events
  • Festival - Happiness, relationship boosts.
  • Good Harvest - Food surplus, coin bonus.
  • Blessing - HP restoration for all.
  • Windfall - Treasury bonus.

  • Same job guild: +10 bonus
  • Socializing together: +5 to +15 per interaction
  • Stealing from someone: -20 penalty
👨‍👩‍👧 Family System
Marriage
  • Requires: Both adults (17+), opposite gender
  • Both unmarried, relationship 60+
  • Not family members
  • Spouse takes partner's family name
Children
  • Birth chance based on couple age/health
  • Child inherits parents' family name
  • Stats partially inherited (with variance)
  • Rare chance to inherit skills
Childhood (Age 0-16)
  • Job: "Child" (no work income)
  • Cannot marry or vote
  • Protected from dangerous quests
  • At 17: assigned adult job
Inheritance
  • On death: coins go to spouse or children
  • If no heirs: coins go to treasury
  • Family name persists through children
👑 Elections & Leadership
Regular Elections
  • Every 5 years
  • All adults can vote
  • Leadership score = REP + INT + traits
  • Winner becomes King
Emergency Elections
  • Triggered when King dies
  • Immediate vote among survivors
  • No term limit for emergency
King Benefits
  • Sets tax policy (trait-based)
  • Influences quest selection
  • Queen consort (spouse) assigned
  • Protected from certain risks
Term Limits
  • Max 3 terms per villager
  • After 3rd term: ineligible
  • Encourages dynasty diversity
💰 Economy & Taxation
Income Sources
  • Work action (job-based pay)
  • Hunting rewards
  • Quest completion
  • Inheritance
Tax System
  • Base rate: 10%
  • King traits modify rate (±8%)
  • Tax Office building: +1% per level
  • Treasury building: +0.5% per level
King Trait Effects on Tax
Greedy: +8% Ambitious: +3% Cautious: +2% Hot-headed: +1% Generous: -4% Wise: -2% Loyal: -2%
