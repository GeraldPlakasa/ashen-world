Details
Systems

  • Auto-generation of 50 initial villagers with randomized stats (ATK, DEF, HP, INT, REP, etc.)
  • Gender-based system (Male/Female)
  • Age progression: 1 year = 90 in-game days
  • Job assignment from 50+ occupations
  • Trait system (~20 traits affecting behavior)
  • Family name inheritance system
  • Immigration system (new villagers can join the village)

Villagers choose actions via AI weights (traits, jobs, needs, buildings).
Core actions
  • Train — increase ATK/DEF
  • Study — increase INT
  • Work — earn coins (taxed)
  • Rest — restore HP
  • Buy Food — reduce hunger
Social / risk actions
  • Buy Gear — items with bonuses
  • Hunt — combat for coins/EXP/REP (taxed)
  • Socialize — improve relationship (1 target)
  • Hangout — group social action
  • Steal — take coins (damages relationships)

  • Hunt system with procedural enemies (tiers: common, elite, legendary)
  • Enemy scaling based on villager level
  • Combat resolution with win/loss/death outcomes
  • Experience and leveling system
  • Starvation damage mechanics
  • Death system integrated with inheritance

  • Dynamic relationship scores (-100 to +120) between villagers
  • Labels: friend, bestfriend, love, rival, enemy
  • Relationship-based actions: socialize, hangout, conflicts
  • Relationship decay/growth over time and interactions

  • Male-female pairing only
  • Love threshold triggers marriage chance
  • Spouse system with mutual tracking
  • Breakup mechanics based on relationship deterioration
  • Prohibition of same-family marriages
  • Widowing system when spouse dies

Birth logic
  • Childbirth system for married couples
  • Fertility windows: mothers 18–42, fathers 18–65
  • Birth cooldown: 45 days
  • Probability influenced by love score + decay by number of children
Children (0–16)
  • Immune to hunger
  • Limited action set (train, study, rest, hangout, socialize)
  • Stat inheritance from parents
  • Trait inheritance with mutation chance
Coming of age (17+)
Automatic job assignment based on stats/traits.

  • Estate distribution on death
  • Heir priority: children → spouse → same-family adults → treasury
  • Player character inheritance: child → sibling succession
  • Automatic transfer of coins to heirs

King / Queen system
  • Elections every 5 years
  • Emergency elections when King dies
  • Voting system with leadership scores
  • Term limits: 3 terms max
  • Dynasty bonus for same-family candidates
  • Queen assigned as King’s spouse
  • Job reassignment for dethroned kings
Assassination mechanics
  • Small daily chance to assassinate enemies
  • Influenced by King traits + village buildings
  • Relationship-driven targets

  • Village bank / treasury
  • Dynamic tax rates (0–35%) based on King traits + building levels
  • Tax collection on work and hunt income
  • Corruption mechanics (King/Queen/Nobles can steal from treasury)

Buildings (12)
  • Marketplace — boosts work income, reduces food cost
  • Library — boosts study effectiveness
  • Barracks — training bonuses, hunt success
  • Granary — reduces hunger consumption
  • Clinic — reduces damage, lowers death rate
  • City Walls — defense bonuses
  • Temple — REP gains, lowers death chance
  • Blacksmith — ATK/DEF bonuses, gear cost reduction
  • Treasury — passive daily interest
  • Royal Court — prestige building
  • Tax Office — increases tax rate
  • Tavern — social bonuses, hangout effectiveness
Mechanics
  • 3 levels per building
  • Construction / upgrade cost scaling
  • Health system (0–100%) with daily decay
  • Auto-repair when damaged below 60%
  • Traits influence building priorities
  • Collapse if health reaches 0

  • Children can’t work/earn; limited actions until age 17
  • Marriage age window: 18–60
  • Fertility: mothers 18–42, fathers 18–65
  • Birth cooldown: 45 days
  • Death archival (graveyard after 25 years dead)
  • One player character per user (duplicate prevention)
  • Same-family marriage prohibition
  • King term limits: 3 terms lifetime

Villagers choose actions with weighted preferences influenced by traits.
  • Brave → more train/hunt
  • Cautious → more study, less steal/hunt
  • Greedy → more work/steal; prioritizes treasury/market
  • Generous → more socialize; prioritizes clinic/granary
  • Wise → more study; prioritizes library
  • Hot-headed → more train/combat
  • Diligent → more work/train
  • Lazy → more rest, less work/train
  • Deceitful → more steal; higher corruption chance
  • Empathic → more socialize
  • …and more

  • Dynamic shop offers with 30+ item types across 3 tiers
  • Tier 1 (common): Weapon, Armor, Tome, Elixir, etc.
  • Tier 2 (rare): Vitality Belt, Mageweave Robe, etc.
  • Tier 3 (legendary): Dragonbone Plate, Voidsteel Greatblade, etc.
  • Permanent stat bonuses (ATK, DEF, HP, INT, REP)
  • Level-based tier weighting
  • Blacksmith building reduces gear cost
