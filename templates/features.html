Game Systems
Detailed Documentation

Earn achievements by reaching milestones. Each achievement grants stat bonuses or unlocks special traits!

Dynasty Founder
  • Have 5 or more children
  • Reward: +15 REP, "Patriarch" trait
Kingmaker
  • Vote for 5 different winning kings
  • Reward: +10 INT
Centurion
  • Reach age 100
  • Reward: +50 HP, "Immortal" trait
Legendary Hunter
  • Win 500 hunts total
  • Reward: +25 ATK, "Hunter" trait
Wealthy
  • Accumulate 10,000 coins
  • Reward: +1,000 coins bonus
Survivor
  • Survive a plague event
  • Reward: +10 DEF
Elder Statesman
  • Become King at age 60+
  • Reward: +20 REP, "Wise" trait
Iron Will
  • Survive with HP below 10, five times
  • Reward: +15 DEF, "Resilient" trait
Questmaster
  • Complete 5 successful quests
  • Reward: +10 ATK, "Veteran" trait

Every 2 years, the King issues a quest. Villagers volunteer based on their traits, stats, and economic conditions. Parties of 2-5 villagers embark on missions with rewards and risks.

Combat Quest
  • Hunt dangerous creatures or bandits
  • Stat focus: ATK
  • Rewards: 150-400 gold
  • Barracks building boosts success
Diplomacy Quest
  • Negotiate with neighboring villages
  • Stat focus: INT
  • Rewards: 100-300 gold
  • Royal Court building boosts success
Exploration Quest
  • Scout ruins, forests, or mountains
  • Stat focus: DEF
  • Rewards: 120-350 gold
  • Library building boosts success
Trade Quest
  • Acquire goods or establish trade routes
  • Stat focus: REP
  • Rewards: 200-500 gold
  • Market building boosts success
Party Formation
  • 2-5 villagers per party
  • Volunteers scored by relevant stats + traits
  • Poor villagers more likely to volunteer
  • King/Queen never participate
King's Influence
  • Brave King: More combat/exploration quests
  • Wise King: More diplomacy/trade quests
  • Greedy King: More trade quests
  • Hot-headed King: More combat quests
Success Calculation (EXTREME)
  • Combat: ~200 avg ATK threshold
  • Diplomacy: ~180 avg INT threshold
  • Exploration: ~190 avg DEF threshold
  • Trade: ~170 avg REP threshold
  • Buildings add +2 per level
  • Success rate: 1-95% based on ratio
Economic Factors
  • Party wealth affects supplies/bribes
  • Trade quests: up to +5 bonus from coins
  • Diplomacy: up to +3 bonus (bribes)
  • Combat/Exploration: up to +2 (supplies)
  • Treasury support: up to +3 bonus
  • Hungry/low HP members reduce success
Gear Rewards
  • 25-40% chance to earn gear on success
  • Best performer receives the gear
  • T1: Battle Trophy, Explorer's Cloak (+3-7)
  • T2: Slayer's Blade, Guardian's Shield (+8-14)
  • T3: Dragonslayer's Edge, Champion's Plate (+14-22)
Difficulty Scaling
  • Quests are extremely difficult
  • Most early-game quests will fail
  • Need high-level veteran warriors
  • Economy and HP matter significantly
  • Random luck factor: ±15%
Outcomes
  • Success: Gold + gear for best performer + XP + stat boosts
  • Failure: HP damage (15-65), possible deaths, treasury loses 50-150 gold
  • Timing: Every 3 years on a random day (Year 3, 6, 9...)
  • Achievement: Complete 5 quests → Questmaster (+10 ATK, "Veteran" trait)

Villagers experience the effects of aging as they grow older. Decay is gradual to allow elders to live longer.

Prime Years (18-69)
  • No age-related penalties
  • Full stat growth potential
  • Normal HP recovery
Early Elder (70-79)
  • 0 to -1 HP per day
  • 2% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~2% yearly natural death chance
Old Age (80-89)
  • -1 to -2 HP per day
  • 5% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~7% yearly natural death chance
Very Old (90-99)
  • -1 to -3 HP per day
  • 10% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~17% yearly natural death chance
Centurion (100+)
  • -2 to -5 HP per day, 15% daily stat decay chance, ~30% yearly death chance
  • Reaching 100 unlocks the Centurion achievement (+50 HP, "Immortal" trait)

Random world events add dynamic challenges and rewards to the village. Events occur once per year on a random day.

☠️ Plague
  • -15-40 HP for 40-55% of villagers
  • Can cause deaths
  • Mitigation: Clinic (-20% severity per level)
🌾 Famine
  • +15-30 hunger for 60-80% of villagers
  • Treasury loses 5-15%
  • Mitigation: Granary (-20% severity per level)
🎉 Festival
  • +2-8 REP, HP restore, hunger reduction
  • Treasury gains 50-150 coins
  • Boost: Temple, Tavern (+15% per level)
⚔️ Invasion
  • Combat damage (20-50 HP), possible deaths
  • Treasury loses 10-25%
  • Mitigation: Barracks (-15%), Walls (-20% per level)
🌻 Good Harvest
  • Reduces hunger for all villagers
  • Bonus coins for nature workers
  • Boost: Granary, Market (+20% per level)
✨ Blessing
  • +25-50 HP heal for all
  • 30% chance of +1-3 ATK/DEF/INT
  • Boost: Temple (+25% per level)
Event Timing
  • Frequency: Once per year, on a random day
  • Event Type: Weighted random (positive events more common)
  • Event notifications show Year for easier tracking

Dynamic weather system that affects gameplay and visuals.

☀️ Sunny Weather (65%)
  • Normal work efficiency
  • Standard hunting conditions
  • Base socializing bonuses
  • Warm golden background glow
🌧️ Rainy Weather (35%)
  • Reduced outdoor activity efficiency
  • Hunting becomes harder
  • Animated rain particles on screen
  • Cool blue background glow
⚙️ Weather Mechanics
  • Roll frequency: Every 5 in-game days
  • Header badge: Current weather shown in dashboard header
  • Visual effects: Background gradient + rain animation
  • API sync: Weather updates in real-time via auto-refresh

🎲 Generation
  • 50 initial villagers with randomized stats
  • Gender-based system (Male/Female)
  • Random age distribution (18-50)
  • Family name assignment
📊 Stats & Attributes
  • HP: Health (0 = death)
  • ATK/DEF: Combat stats
  • INT: Intelligence
  • REP: Reputation
  • Hunger: Food level (100 = starving)
⏰ Time System
  • 1 year = 90 in-game days
  • Villagers age +1 at year boundary
  • Natural death at old age (70+)
🚪 Immigration
  • New villagers can join daily
  • Triggered when population low
  • Random stats and traits
💼 Jobs (50+ Occupations)

Farmer, Blacksmith, Merchant, Scholar, Soldier, Hunter, Healer, Priest, Noble, Knight, Guard, Baker, Tailor, Mason, Carpenter, Miner, Shepherd, Fisherman, Brewer, Innkeeper, Scribe, Alchemist, Bard, Thief, Assassin, and many more...

Core Actions
  • Train — ATK/DEF
  • Study — INT
  • Work — Coins (taxed)
  • Rest — HP
  • Buy Food — Hunger
Social / Risk
  • Buy Gear — Stat items
  • Hunt — Combat
  • Socialize — +Relationship
  • Hangout — Group social
  • Steal — Risk action

🎯 Hunt System
  • Procedural enemy generation
  • Common: Basic creatures
  • Elite: Stronger foes (2x stats)
  • Legendary: Rare bosses (3x stats)
⚡ Combat Resolution
  • ATK vs enemy DEF calculation
  • Win: XP + coins + small scratch damage (10%)
  • Loss: Significant HP damage
  • Death: HP reaches 0
📈 Leveling & HP
  • XP from combat, training, work
  • Level up grants ATK/DEF/HP bonuses
  • HP has no upper limit — grows with level
  • Higher level = stronger enemies
💀 Starvation
  • Hunger 100+ = HP damage/day
  • Buy food to reduce hunger
  • Children immune to hunger

📊 Relationship Scores
  • Range: -100 to +120
  • Grows through socialize (+5 to +15) / hangout (+3 to +10)
  • Minimal decay (15% chance of -1 to -2)
  • Traits affect relationship gains
🏷️ Relationship Labels
  • Enemy: -100 to -50
  • Rival: -50 to -10
  • Friend: +30 to +60
  • Best Friend: +60 to +85
  • Love: +85 to +120
💥 Relationship Effects
  • Love: Enables marriage proposal
  • Enemy: May trigger assassination attempts
  • Family: Base relationship bonus for relatives

Marriage Requirements
  • Love threshold: 70+ relationship score
  • Age: Both partners must be 18+
  • Family: Different families only
  • Status: Both must be unmarried
Spouse System
  • Mutual spouse tracking (spouseId)
  • King's spouse becomes Queen automatically
  • Widowing on spouse death
  • Remarriage allowed after widowing
Breakup & Divorce
  • Relationship decay can trigger breakup
  • Negative relationship scores lead to divorce
  • Children remain linked to both parents

Birth Logic
  • Mothers 18–50, fathers 18–75
  • Birth cooldown: 45 days
  • Probability by love score
Children (0–16)
  • Immune to hunger
  • Limited actions
  • Stat/trait inheritance

  • Estate distribution on death
  • Priority: children → spouse → family → treasury
  • Player character succession

👑 King System
  • Scheduled: Elections every 5 years
  • Emergency: Immediate election if King dies
  • Term limits: 3 terms lifetime max
  • Dynasty bonus: Same family gets advantage
🗳️ Election Mechanics
  • Voters: All villagers age 16+
  • Candidates: Adults 18+, prefer males
  • Results: Vote count + percentage shown
  • Tie-breaker: Leadership → REP → INT → Level
📊 Leadership Score
  • INT × 1.2 + REP × 0.9 + Level × 2.0
  • Trait bonuses: Wise (+14), Generous (+8)
  • Trait penalties: Greedy (-10), Deceitful (-12)
  • Age preference: 22-55 years optimal
🗡️ Assassination
  • Daily chance to assassinate enemies
  • Influenced by traits (Deceitful, Brave)
  • Walls building reduces success rate
  • Royal Court increases detection

🏦 Treasury System
  • Village-wide treasury balance
  • Funds building construction
  • Treasury building adds daily interest
  • Events can add/remove treasury funds
📈 Tax System
  • Rate: 0–35% (set by King's traits)
  • Applied to: Work income, hunt loot
  • Generous King: Lower tax (5-10%)
  • Greedy King: Higher tax (25-35%)
💸 Corruption
  • King/Queen/Nobles can skim treasury
  • Daily chance based on traits
  • Greedy: Higher corruption chance
  • Tracked yearly in Leaderboard
💵 Income Sources
  • Work: Base coins by job
  • Hunt: Loot from defeated enemies
  • Steal: Risk action for coins
  • Inheritance: From deceased relatives

Economic Buildings
  • Marketplace — work income, harvest boost
  • Treasury — passive interest (0.3%/level/day)
  • Tax Office — adjusts tax rate
  • Blacksmith — gear quality
Military Buildings
  • Barracks — training, invasion -15%/level
  • Walls — defense, invasion -20%/level
  • Royal Court — prestige, assassination detection
Survival Buildings
  • Clinic — survival, plague -20%/level
  • Granary — hunger, famine -20%/level
  • Library — study boost
Social Buildings
  • Temple — REP gains, blessing +25%/level
  • Tavern — social, festival +15%/level
🔧 Mechanics
  • 3 levels per building (upgrade costs increase)
  • Health system: 0–100%, decays over time
  • Auto-repair: Triggers below 60% health
  • Event mitigations: Buildings reduce negative event impacts

Each villager has 1-2 traits that influence their daily actions, leadership potential, and social interactions. Traits are assigned at birth (inherited or random) or earned through achievements.

⚔️ Combat & Physical
🦁 Brave
  • Actions: +60% train, +60% hunt, +30% buy gear
  • Leadership: +6 score
  • Combat: More likely to engage enemies
🔥 Hot-headed
  • Actions: +40% train, +40% hunt, +30% steal
  • Leadership: -8 score (impulsive)
  • Social: May damage relationships
⚡ Reckless
  • Actions: +60% train, +60% hunt, +30% buy gear
  • Leadership: -6 score (careless)
  • Risk: Takes dangerous actions often
🛡️ Protective
  • Actions: +60% train, +60% hunt, +30% buy gear
  • Social: Defends family and friends
  • Combat: Prioritizes defense
📚 Intellectual
🧠 Wise
  • Actions: +80% study, +40% socialize
  • Leadership: +14 score (highest bonus)
  • Elections: Highly valued by voters
💡 Clever
  • Actions: +80% study, +40% socialize, +30% hangout
  • Work: More efficient at intellectual jobs
  • Combat: Better tactical decisions
🔍 Curious
  • Actions: +80% study, +40% socialize, +30% hangout
  • Learning: Faster skill acquisition
  • Social: Engages with many villagers
🐢 Cautious
  • Actions: +40% study, +20% rest, -30% hunt, -20% steal
  • Leadership: +2 score
  • Risk: Avoids dangerous situations
💬 Social & Personality
💝 Generous
  • Actions: +40% buy food, +40% socialize, +50% hangout, -20% steal
  • Leadership: +8 score

🤝 Loyal

⏳ Patient

💼 Work & Ambition

📊 Trait Inheritance
  • Children: 60% chance to inherit one parent's trait
  • Random: 40% chance for a completely random trait
  • Achievement: Special traits earned through gameplay milestones
  • Mutation: Rare chance for opposing trait (e.g., Lazy parent → Diligent child)

Skills are rare abilities that villagers can be born with. They provide stat bonuses and influence job selection.

Birth & Inheritance
  • 12% chance to have at least one skill
  • 20% chance for second skill
  • 25% chance to inherit each parent's skill
  • Max 2 skills per villager
Action Bonuses
  • Skills boost gains during actions
  • COMBAT: +20% train ATK/DEF
  • CRAFT: +20% work income
  • KNOWLEDGE: +25% study INT
  • SOCIAL: +25% relationship gains
Job Influence
  • 75% chance to follow skill affinity
  • Skills suggest matching professions
  • Falls back to trait-based
COMBAT Skills (boost train/hunt)
  • Bladesong - +30% ATK training, Soldier/Guard
  • Hawkeye - +20% ATK/DEF, Archer/Ranger
  • Bloodrage - +25% hunt power (rare)
  • Bulwark - +35% DEF training, -15% damage
  • Battlemaster - +15% EXP gain (rare)
CRAFT Skills (boost work)
  • Forgeblessed - +30% work income, Blacksmith
  • Transmutation - +20% coins, Alchemist
  • Gilded Hands - +35% work income, Jeweler
  • Artificer - +20% coins (rare)
  • Brewcraft - +20% coins, Brewer
SOCIAL Skills (boost socialize)
  • Silver Tongue - +45% relationship, Merchant
  • Commanding Presence - +25% REP (rare)
  • Dealmaker - +25% relations, +20% work
  • Dreadgaze - +25% relations, Guard
  • Heartspark - +40% relations (rare)
SURVIVAL Skills (boost hunt/rest)
  • Pathfinder - +20% hunt power, Hunter
  • Herbweaver - +20% rest HP, -3 hunger
  • Iron Constitution - +30% rest HP, -15% dmg
  • Wayfarer - +10% hunt power, Scout
  • Beastbond - +10% hunt, Shepherd
KNOWLEDGE Skills (boost study)
  • Lorekeeper - +40% INT study, Scholar/Scribe
  • Arcane Attunement - +35% INT, +10% EXP (rare)
  • Chirurgeon - +25% INT, +25% rest HP, Healer
  • Polyglot - +25% INT, +20% EXP, Diplomat
  • Chronicle - +25% INT study, Scholar/Advisor
Rarity: Common (60%), Uncommon (30%), Rare (10%)
Note: Skills provide bonuses during actions, not flat stat increases.
