Earn achievements by reaching milestones. Each achievement grants stat bonuses or unlocks special traits!

Dynasty Founder
  • Have 5 or more children
  • Reward: +15 REP, "Patriarch" trait
Kingmaker
  • Vote for 3 different winning kings
  • Reward: +10 INT
Centurion
  • Reach age 100
  • Reward: +50 HP, "Immortal" trait
Legendary Hunter
  • Win 500 hunts total
  • Reward: +25 ATK, "Hunter" trait
Wealthy
  • Accumulate 10,000 coins
  • Reward: +1,000 coins bonus
Survivor
  • Survive a plague event
  • Reward: +10 DEF
Elder Statesman
  • Become King at age 60+
  • Reward: +20 REP, "Wise" trait
Iron Will
  • Survive with HP below 10, five times
  • Reward: +15 DEF, "Resilient" trait

Villagers experience the effects of aging as they grow older. Decay is gradual to allow elders to live longer.

Prime Years (18-69)
  • No age-related penalties
  • Full stat growth potential
  • Normal HP recovery
Early Elder (70-79)
  • 0 to -1 HP per day
  • 2% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~2% yearly natural death chance
Old Age (80-89)
  • -1 to -2 HP per day
  • 5% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~7% yearly natural death chance
Very Old (90-99)
  • -1 to -3 HP per day
  • 10% daily chance of stat decay
  • ~17% yearly natural death chance
Centurion (100+)
  • -2 to -5 HP per day, 15% daily stat decay chance, ~30% yearly death chance
  • Reaching 100 unlocks the Centurion achievement (+50 HP, "Immortal" trait)

Random world events add dynamic challenges and rewards to the village. Events occur once per year on a random day.

☠️ Plague
  • -15-40 HP for 40-55% of villagers
  • Can cause deaths
  • Mitigation: Clinic (-20% severity per level)
🌾 Famine
  • +15-30 hunger for 60-80% of villagers
  • Treasury loses 5-15%
  • Mitigation: Granary (-20% severity per level)
🎉 Festival
  • +2-8 REP, HP restore, hunger reduction
  • Treasury gains 50-150 coins
  • Boost: Temple, Tavern (+15% per level)
⚔️ Invasion
  • Combat damage (20-50 HP), possible deaths
  • Treasury loses 10-25%
  • Mitigation: Barracks (-15%), Walls (-20% per level)
🌻 Good Harvest
  • Reduces hunger for all villagers
  • Bonus coins for nature workers
  • Boost: Granary, Market (+20% per level)
✨ Blessing
  • +25-50 HP heal for all
  • 30% chance of +1-3 ATK/DEF/INT
  • Boost: Temple (+25% per level)
Event Timing
  • Frequency: Once per year, on a random day
  • Event Type: Weighted random (positive events more common)
  • Event notifications show Year for easier tracking

Dynamic weather system that affects gameplay and visuals.

☀️ Sunny Weather (65%)
  • Normal work efficiency
  • Standard hunting conditions
  • Base socializing bonuses
  • Warm golden background glow
🌧️ Rainy Weather (35%)
  • Reduced outdoor activity efficiency
  • Hunting becomes harder
  • Animated rain particles on screen
  • Cool blue background glow
⚙️ Weather Mechanics
  • Roll frequency: Every 5 in-game days
  • Header badge: Current weather shown in dashboard header
  • Visual effects: Background gradient + rain animation
  • API sync: Weather updates in real-time via auto-refresh

🎲 Generation
  • 50 initial villagers with randomized stats
  • Gender-based system (Male/Female)
  • Random age distribution (18-50)
  • Family name assignment
📊 Stats & Attributes
  • HP: Health (0 = death)
  • ATK/DEF: Combat stats
  • INT: Intelligence
  • REP: Reputation
  • Hunger: Food level (100 = starving)
⏰ Time System
  • 1 year = 90 in-game days
  • Villagers age +1 at year boundary
  • Natural death at old age (70+)
🚪 Immigration
  • New villagers can join daily
  • Triggered when population low
  • Random stats and traits
💼 Jobs (50+ Occupations)

Farmer, Blacksmith, Merchant, Scholar, Soldier, Hunter, Healer, Priest, Noble, Knight, Guard, Baker, Tailor, Mason, Carpenter, Miner, Shepherd, Fisherman, Brewer, Innkeeper, Scribe, Alchemist, Bard, Thief, Assassin, and many more...

Core Actions
  • Train — ATK/DEF
  • Study — INT
  • Work — Coins (taxed)
  • Rest — HP
  • Buy Food — Hunger
Social / Risk
  • Buy Gear — Stat items
  • Hunt — Combat
  • Socialize — +Relationship
  • Hangout — Group social
  • Steal — Risk action

🎯 Hunt System
  • Procedural enemy generation
  • Common: Basic creatures
  • Elite: Stronger foes (2x stats)
  • Legendary: Rare bosses (3x stats)
⚡ Combat Resolution
  • ATK vs enemy DEF calculation
  • Win: XP + coins + small scratch damage (10%)
  • Loss: Significant HP damage
  • Death: HP reaches 0
📈 Leveling & HP
  • XP from combat, training, work
  • Level up grants ATK/DEF/HP bonuses
  • HP has no upper limit — grows with level
  • Higher level = stronger enemies
💀 Starvation
  • Hunger 100+ = HP damage/day
  • Buy food to reduce hunger
  • Children immune to hunger

📊 Relationship Scores
  • Range: -100 to +120
  • Grows through socialize (+5 to +15) / hangout (+3 to +10)
  • Minimal decay (15% chance of -1 to -2)
  • Traits affect relationship gains
🏷️ Relationship Labels
  • Enemy: -100 to -50
  • Rival: -50 to -10
  • Friend: +30 to +60
  • Best Friend: +60 to +85
  • Love: +85 to +120
💥 Relationship Effects
  • Love: Enables marriage proposal
  • Enemy: May trigger assassination attempts
  • Family: Base relationship bonus for relatives

Marriage Requirements
  • Love threshold: 70+ relationship score
  • Age: Both partners must be 18+
  • Family: Different families only
  • Status: Both must be unmarried
Spouse System
  • Mutual spouse tracking (spouseId)
  • King's spouse becomes Queen automatically
  • Widowing on spouse death
  • Remarriage allowed after widowing
Breakup & Divorce
  • Relationship decay can trigger breakup
  • Negative relationship scores lead to divorce
  • Children remain linked to both parents

Birth Logic
  • Mothers 18–50, fathers 18–75
  • Birth cooldown: 45 days
  • Probability by love score
Children (0–16)
  • Immune to hunger
  • Limited actions
  • Stat/trait inheritance

  • Estate distribution on death
  • Priority: children → spouse → family → treasury
  • Player character succession

👑 King System
  • Scheduled: Elections every 5 years
  • Emergency: Immediate election if King dies
  • Term limits: 3 terms lifetime max
  • Dynasty bonus: Same family gets advantage
🗳️ Election Mechanics
  • Voters: All villagers age 16+
  • Candidates: Adults 18+, prefer males
  • Results: Vote count + percentage shown
  • Tie-breaker: Leadership → REP → INT → Level
📊 Leadership Score
  • INT × 1.2 + REP × 0.9 + Level × 2.0
  • Trait bonuses: Wise (+14), Generous (+8)
  • Trait penalties: Greedy (-10), Deceitful (-12)
  • Age preference: 22-55 years optimal
🗡️ Assassination
  • Daily chance to assassinate enemies
  • Influenced by traits (Deceitful, Brave)
  • Walls building reduces success rate
  • Royal Court increases detection

🏦 Treasury System
  • Village-wide treasury balance
  • Funds building construction
  • Treasury building adds daily interest
  • Events can add/remove treasury funds
📈 Tax System
  • Rate: 0–35% (set by King's traits)
  • Applied to: Work income, hunt loot
  • Generous King: Lower tax (5-10%)
  • Greedy King: Higher tax (25-35%)
💸 Corruption
  • King/Queen/Nobles can skim treasury
  • Daily chance based on traits
  • Greedy: Higher corruption chance
  • Tracked yearly in Leaderboard
💵 Income Sources
  • Work: Base coins by job
  • Hunt: Loot from defeated enemies
  • Steal: Risk action for coins
  • Inheritance: From deceased relatives

Economic Buildings
  • Marketplace — work income, harvest boost
  • Treasury — passive interest (0.3%/level/day)
  • Tax Office — adjusts tax rate
  • Blacksmith — gear quality
Military Buildings
  • Barracks — training, invasion -15%/level
  • Walls — defense, invasion -20%/level
  • Royal Court — prestige, assassination detection
Survival Buildings
  • Clinic — survival, plague -20%/level
  • Granary — hunger, famine -20%/level
  • Library — study boost
Social Buildings
  • Temple — REP gains, blessing +25%/level
  • Tavern — social, festival +15%/level
🔧 Mechanics
  • 3 levels per building (upgrade costs increase)
  • Health system: 0–100%, decays over time
  • Auto-repair: Triggers below 60% health
  • Event mitigations: Buildings reduce negative event impacts
